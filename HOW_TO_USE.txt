### 📸 HOW TO USE

**1.** Take a screenshot of **ONLY team A's nicknames** — places 1 to 10.  
Hotkey: **`SHIFT + WIN + S`** → select the area → screenshot goes to clipboard automatically.

**2.** Take a screenshot of team B **the same way** (**`SHIFT + WIN + S`**).

**3.** Click the **`⚖️ Compare`** button.

**4.** Done — the comparison table appears at the bottom of the window.  
Click **`📋 Copy to clipboard`** to save a screenshot of the result for Discord.

---

### ⚙️ BUTTONS

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **📸 Scan** | Recognizes the latest screenshot from clipboard and adds a team |
| **✏️ Manual Input** | Enter nicknames manually (if OCR failed) |
| **⚖️ Compare** | Compares the last two teams |
| **🧹 Clear Teams** | Resets the team buffer |
| **📋 Show Log** | Shows detailed analysis log |
| **🔊 / 🔇** | Enable/disable sound |
| **📋 Copy to Clipboard** | Copies the result for sharing |

---

### 🎨 COLORS

- 🟢 **Weak** — low stats
- 🟡 **Below average**
- 🟠 **Average**
- 🔴 **Strong**
- 🟣 **Elite**

---

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT

- The screenshot must contain **only nicknames** (no extra panels, buttons, or chat).
- If OCR fails — use **`✏️ Manual Input`**.
- If Windows Defender flags the `.exe` — it's a **false positive**, click "More info" → "Run anyway".
